Top 3 Must-Visit Destinations in Hong Kong

1. Hong Kong Disneyland

Location: Lantau Island, Hong Kong

Located on Lantau Island, Hong Kong Disneyland offers magical lands such as Toy Story Land, Grizzly Gulch, and Mystic Point. It is a favorite destination for families and Disney fans looking for unforgettable parades and spectacular evening fireworks displays.

2. Ocean Park Hong Kong

Location: Southern District, Hong Kong

Divided into two main areas—the Waterfront and the Summit—connected by a scenic cable car and the Ocean Express train. It features a thrilling combination of rollercoasters, giant pandas, and massive marine aquarium exhibits.

3. Victoria Peak (The Peak)

Location: Hong Kong Island

Victoria Peak provides visitors with breathtaking panoramic views of Hong Kong Island, Victoria Harbour, and the Kowloon peninsula. Riding the historic Peak Tram up to the summit is an adventure in itself during your visit.
